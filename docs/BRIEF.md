# BRIEF: Emmanuel Lutheran Global Seminary (rebuild of www.elgs.org)

Interviewed. Answers below are the owner's words, verbatim. Lines marked
**Authored** are decisions made by the builder where the answer left room.

## The interview

1. **Subject.** "A rebuid of www.elgs.org"
2. **Belief.** "That ELGS is the most innovative deliverer of Lutheran pastoral
   formation and preparation for ministry."
3. **Action.** "Prospective students should submit a contact form for a follow
   up conversation. Prospective donors should click the Support or Give Now
   button and make a contribution, ideally a recurring contribution."
4. **Vibe.** "modern, humble, sophisticated, worldly, ethical"
   (No references from other media were given.)
5. **Journey.** "What do we do best, who do we do it for, why choose us as a
   seminary or why donate to us."
6. **Energy.** "Calm and ethical"
7. **Feeling and the one moment.** "Innovative, flexible, best fit, unique
   customization for each student."
8. **Signature.** "We can be trusted"
9. **Style range.** "Must fit the modern innovative seminary vibe."
10. **Structure.** "one unbroken world the scroll flies through with a unique
    donor pathway that is always visible"
11. **Assets.** "Use images that are already on the existing website. Describe
    optimal images that are not yet on website. We may have in our library or
    can create."

## Authored decisions

- **Grammar: continuous world (worldflight).** The owner chose it in question
  10. The world is one sanctuary: the camera starts under the stained glass,
  passes the lectern, the Word, the pews, arrives at a real commencement, sees
  the wider church, reads the record, and walks out the door to the cross
  under the Naples sky.
- **No generation key was available and the owner asked for existing images.**
  Every leg is a real photograph from elgs.org with a slow camera move rendered
  across it. This is a flight of dissolving stills, not shot footage. See the
  shot list in the report for what would replace them.
- **Aesthetic family: editorial-modern on a deep ink ground.** Ink navy, bone
  type, and the gold of the seminary seal as the single accent. Brand colours,
  not the cream-and-brass default.
- **One label per intent.** Students: "Start a conversation". Donors: "Give now".

## Journey (beats)

```
1  Claim        formation rebuilt around the student
2  Method       you learn where you lead (what we do best)
3  Fit          no two paths are the same (customization)
4  Who          the called who cannot leave
5  Proof        real graduates, real commencement            PEAK
6  Reach        the same formation, wherever the church is
7  Trust        don't take our word for it (why choose, why give)
8  Commitment   two ways out the door: a conversation, or a gift
```

## The feeling curve

```
1  Stillness     coloured light through the chapel gable, one sentence, nothing else moving
2  Recognition   a hand resting on a lectern: the work you already do, named as the classroom
3  Agency        the visitor taps what they already do and the page answers "use that"
4  Belonging     the empty pews, held quiet, and a line that describes their actual life
5  Pride         the wood and bokeh give way to a real hooding in bright daylight (PEAK)
6  Breadth       the same gown, another continent
7  Assurance     the room goes dark and calm; every claim hands over its outside source
8  Resolve       outside, the cross under open sky, two plain actions, and the page stops
```

## The peak

Leg 5, commencement. The sentence a visitor would say to a friend:

> You drift through this quiet chapel, and then you are standing at an actual
> graduation watching a real student get hooded.

It gets the longest leg (1.95vh against 1.3vh for every other leg), the only
bright, saturated, populated frame after four quiet interior details, and the
quietest leg on the page directly before it.

## Tell-someone sentence

It's the site where **the giving panel walks the whole chapel with you, and
every claim the seminary makes hands you the outside source to check it.**

## Signature move: the open book

A persistent donor panel, present on every frame. It does three things no kit
device does:

1. Holds the one donor action ("Give now") in the same place the whole flight.
2. Translates a monthly gift into months of one seminarian's tuition per year,
   computed live from the published $300 monthly cap. An illustration of scale,
   labelled as such, never a designation of the gift.
3. Keeps a ledger. As the visitor passes each claim, its source is stamped into
   the panel ("Sources you can check: 3 of 7"). Opening it lists every claim
   with a link to the outside record, and marks which are independent and which
   are the seminary's own published figures.

## Authored silence

Leg 4 (the pews) carries a short, small block and nothing else. It is the quiet
before the peak, not dead scroll.
